# Loop literature coverage re-audit — 2026-10-15

The original 248-paper version was incomplete. A second pass found concrete omissions in both the existing discovery set and independent official-venue searches. This document records the correction and the finite boundary that was actually checked; it is not a claim that the entire research literature has been exhausted.

## Final catalog and count convention

- **264 main-catalog papers:** 262 date-indexed papers and 2 confirmed ICLR 2026 papers whose first-public dates remain unresolved.
- **16 records added to the main catalog** relative to the original 248. This includes four previously mentioned candidates promoted to formal rows after mechanism or publication evidence was resolved: CHERRY, SAI, ChainGPT, and MoDr.
- **7 earlier preprints with verified 2025 venues**, separately listed and excluded from the main count. This re-audit added AlgoFormer, Transformers Handle Endogeneity, and CoTFormer to that appendix.
- Month-only publication evidence remains month-only. A publisher indexing date is labeled explicitly; an ICLR acceptance date is never substituted for an original public date.

## What the first pass missed

The first pass used broad arXiv keywords and two community discovery lists, but its source-by-source reconciliation was unfinished. In the 323-item discovery snapshot, 54 recent records did not match the original main table: 15 had earlier exclusion decisions, 33 had no explicit completed decision, and 6 were known missing or date-limited records. Those 33 candidates were then screened against live primary abstracts: 31 were excluded under the Transformer-focused scope and 2 (CAIR and Q-DEQ) were verified in full text as shared-Transformer recurrence.

Other omissions came from official ACL, CVPR, ICLR, TMLR, and PMLR searches; titles that did not contain “Loop”; stale discovery aliases; and stopping at an abstract when the mechanism required a full-text check. Exact-day uncertainty was also an overly strict reason to keep recognized conference papers out of formal rows.

## Search boundary and limitations

The independent pass searched arXiv, ACL/EACL Anthology 2025–2026, ICLR proceedings, PMLR/ICML/AISTATS, TMLR/OpenReview, CVF/CVPR, and IEEE publication records using looped / recursive / universal Transformer, recurrent depth, weight tying, depth-wise sharing, adaptive recurrence, and related exact titles. Papers were compared by identifiers, aliases, mechanisms, and source versions; repeated acronyms such as LoopCD were not assumed to denote duplicate works.

- This was a targeted search and candidate reconciliation, **not a full crawl of every venue, citation graph, thesis repository, or unpublished manuscript**. No numerical recall percentage can be supported.
- The 42 older discovery records outside the main date window were not all re-read for later venue history. The earlier-venue appendix is a selected aid for the requested 2025 reading scope.
- Most initial screening uses primary abstracts; ambiguous positive mechanisms received selected full-text checks. An exclusion is under this catalog's scope, not a claim that a paper has no connection to iterative computation.
- ChainGPT and MoDr have official ICLR 2026 records, but their original public dates remain unresolved because of OpenReview access challenges. They are retained and counted, with that uncertainty visible.
- The October 15 Preprints.org survey's claimed corpus size was not independently validated; its full corpus was unavailable in this re-audit. It was not used as evidence of exhaustive coverage.
- Source dates, model results, and code availability can change. New submissions and aliases require another dated audit; the current count is not a completeness guarantee.
